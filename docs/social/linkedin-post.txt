Testing with multiple accounts shouldn't mean juggling browser windows and constantly logging in and out.

That frustration led me to build Talos: an open-source browser workspace for QA designers and testers.

Each account gets its own isolated session. Your application's tabs and that account's Gmail, Outlook or other webmail can sit together in a named group. Switch accounts without losing your place, or open more tabs that deliberately share the same login.

It keeps the familiar browser controls and Chromium DevTools, with a few focused additions: a Playwright locator picker, an API tester that can use the current account's session, and opt-in live Playwright integration.

The 1.0 release candidate has downloads for Windows, macOS and Linux. No Docker required.

I've attached a short walkthrough using dummy accounts. Talos is MIT-licensed, and I'd love feedback from other testers: what would make multi-account QA easier for you?

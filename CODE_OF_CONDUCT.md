# Community conduct

Talos welcomes testers, designers and developers of all backgrounds and levels
of experience. Be respectful, patient and specific. Critique ideas and code;
help people reproduce problems and learn from feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks and
publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not post
credentials, browser profiles or private messages in issues or discussions.

This policy applies to Talos repositories, discussions, reviews and other
project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock threads,
warn participants, or temporarily or permanently exclude repeat or serious
offenders. Decisions should be proportionate and protect affected people.

Report violations to the maintainer, [George Persson](https://github.com/GeorgePersson).
For confidential reports, request a private contact channel without posting
identifying details publicly. Maintainers will keep reports confidential as
far as practical and explain moderation decisions to the people involved.
